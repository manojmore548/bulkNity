Privacy Policy
1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide during registration, including your business name, contact details, and role (Manufacturer, Wholesaler, Retailer). This helps us verify your account and provide role-specific features.

2. How We Use Your Information

To facilitate business connections, manage orders, and improve our social feed experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

BulkNity uses industry-standard encryption to protect your transaction data and private messages.

Terms & Conditions
1. Account Eligibility

By using BulkNity, you represent that you are a legitimate business entity or professional within the clothing industry.

2. User Conduct

Users must not post fraudulent items or engage in spam behavior. BulkNity reserves the right to suspend accounts that violate professional standards.

3. Platform Role

BulkNity is a facilitator. We provide the tools for connection, but the actual fulfillment of bulk orders is the responsibility of the connecting parties.

Refund Policy
1. B2B Transactions

Refunds for bulk purchases are handled directly between the buyer (Retailer/Wholesaler) and the seller (Manufacturer/Wholesaler) based on their specific agreement.

2. Digital Services

Any premium subscription or advertisement credits purchased within the app are non-refundable unless specified otherwise by local law.

3. Dispute Resolution

BulkNity provides a direct messaging platform to resolve disputes. We encourage all users to document their order details clearly within the app.
